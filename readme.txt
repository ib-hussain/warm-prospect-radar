Warm Prospect Radar — central full-workspace milestone

1. Acquire public business sources, prioritising About and company information.
2. Extract structured business, contact, social, GICS, product and technology data.
3. Preserve raw versioned snapshots locally and important structured records in Supabase.
4. Review explainable likelihood and prospect scores in the Flask interface.
5. Draft, review and approve outreach with dry-run or explicitly configured live delivery.
6. Ask grounded questions over the central database and optionally refresh records daily.
7. Use global feature controls; there are no application users or per-user records.

Wikipedia sample loading, outreach providers, Docker, Windows/WSL commands, tests,
